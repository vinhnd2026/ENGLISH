Review — What this lesson tests

This consolidation lesson has no new theory; it tests the four areas you have already studied:

  • Time expressions (the Time expressions lesson): by / until, in a moment, Once upon a time, the other day, now and again, lately…
  • Relative clauses (the Relative clauses lesson): who, which, that, whose, whom; defining and non-defining clauses.
  • Prepositions and prepositional phrases: in favour of, in case of, by chance, for sale, in private, in difficulties, out of work, by surprise, by heart, on strike…
  • Linking words and sentence structures: although / despite / in spite of, so … that, so as to, even though.
By the time you've done the shopping, I'll be ready.
That's the boy whose sister sits behind me at school.
Quite by chance I noticed that the house was for sale.
Susan was so tired that she decided to go to bed.

⭐ Key points

  1. By + a time point = at the latest by that time; until = continuing right up to that time.
  2. Relative clauses: whose shows possession; whom is an object form (formal, often after a preposition: the man from whom…); which for things, who for people; that can replace who/which in defining clauses.
  3. Despite / in spite of + noun or V-ing; although / even though + a full clause.
  4. So + adjective/adverb + that… and so many/much + noun + that…: “to such a degree that”.
  5. Fixed phrases to learn by heart: by chance, for sale, in private, in difficulties, out of work, take someone by surprise, by heart, on strike, in the way, to a great extent.
