Grammar 22 — Articles

Articles: a/an · the · Zero article

1. The indefinite article: a/an

a) First mention

Use a/an when the listener does not know which one you mean — the person or thing is mentioned for the first time.

I've just had a great idea.
I'll give you a call next week.

b) One of a group

A/an can also mean “any one of a group”.

Shall we choose a book from this catalogue?

c) With a descriptive adjective

When a noun has a descriptive adjective in front of it, we often use a/an.

Cairo is a very big city.
It's a beautiful day.

d) Jobs

Use a/an in front of jobs.

Peter is a truck driver.

e) Fractions, groups and large numbers

Numbers are often expressed like this:

one and a half kilos · a dozen eggs · a hundred envelopes

f) Meaning “per”

In expressions of price, speed and frequency, a/an means per.

Julie earns £500 a week.

2. The definite article: the

a) Already mentioned

Use the when the person or thing was mentioned in an earlier sentence.

There is a bedroom and a living room. The bedroom is quite large.

b) Clear which one is meant

Even at first mention, use the if the situation makes it clear which one is meant.

Can you pass the marmalade?
My life changed completely after the war.

c) The only one there is

The moon is full tonight.

d) Noun with a descriptive phrase

A phrase after the noun can make it definite. Note: London Bridge but the Tower of London.

This is the man I told you about.

e) National groups

The British drink far too much tea.

f) Classes of people

The rich get richer and the poor get poorer.

g) One example representing a class

The lion is fast disappearing.

h) Musical instruments

After play with a musical instrument, use the.

I can't play the piano but I can play the guitar.

i) Geographical names

Use the with oceans, seas, rivers, canals and regions, and with country names that are plural or contain a common noun.

the Thames · the North Sea · the Arctic · the Netherlands · the People's Republic of China

j) Superlatives, ordinals, the same, the only

the best · the first · the same · the only one

k) The media

I went to the cinema.

3. The zero article — no article

a) General statements: uncountable and plural nouns

When talking about things in general, use no article.

Give peace a chance.
All he talks about is cars.

b) Most place names

Use no article with most continents, countries, states, islands, mountains, lakes, cities, parks, streets, squares, bridges, palaces, castles, cathedrals, stations and airports.

We live in France.
We took the train from Paddington Station to Bath.

c) Companies, years, months, days, special times of year

She works for Lufthansa.
I'll see you in January.

d) Meals in general

But for a specific meal, use the: The lunch I had at Cafe Sol was good value.

It's time for lunch.

e) Unique jobs

For a job that only one person can hold, the article is often left out (the is also possible).

Jim is chairman of the company.

f) Preposition + place: purpose matters more than the place

With places like bed, church, class, court, hospital, prison, school, university: no the when the activity/purpose is meant; the when the building or place itself is meant.

Sally is in prison. / Sally is in the prison.

g) Transport with by

After by, use no article. But with in/on an article is needed: in a car, on a bus.

We went there by car. / We went there in the car that Alex borrowed.

⭐ Key points

  1. With some buildings and places, using the or not changes the meaning: Helen is at school (learning there — the purpose) is different from Helen is at the school (inside the building — the place).
  2. The shows that something has already been mentioned or is clear from the context: in the report The two men have since been recaptured, the signals that the two men appeared earlier in the report.
  3. Many article uses are idiomatic — learn them as fixed phrases, for example: Diana works as a graphic designer.
