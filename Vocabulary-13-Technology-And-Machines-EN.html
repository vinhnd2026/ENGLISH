1. Machines and how they work

engine — the part of a vehicle or machine that produces the power to make it move (a powerful engine); machine — a piece of equipment in general.

electric — worked by electricity (an electric toothbrush); electrical — to do with electricity in general (electrical equipment).

switch — the thing you press or move to turn something on or off; plug — the thing you push into a socket. To get light, you look for the switch.

notebook — a small, portable computer you can carry to work; desktop PC — a computer that stays on a desk.

out of order — broken, not working (often on a notice on a lift or machine); out of work — unemployed.

go — (of a machine) to start or move: the washing machine won't go; work — to function properly: this torch doesn't work.

power cut — a period when the electricity supply stops. factory — a building where goods are made; industry — a whole area of production.

invent — to create something that has never existed before; discover — to find something that was already there.

This is a small car, but it has a powerful engine.
You can't use the lift. It's out of order.
The lights have gone out. It must be a power cut.
Who exactly invented the computer?

2. Computers

cursor — the flashing mark on the screen that shows where text will appear.

highlight — to mark text as chosen (hold down the left mouse button and drag the mouse over the text).

reboot — to start a computer again, for example after it crashes. icon — a small picture on the screen; to open a document, click on the relevant icon.

copy and paste — to copy text and put it into another document. scroll down — to move down the screen to see what is below.

pull-down menu — a list of options on the toolbar at the top of the screen. website — a place on the Internet you go to for information; search engine — a program that searches for information when you type in a key word; download — to copy a program from the Internet onto your own computer.

To open a document, click on the relevant icon.
If your computer crashes, you may have to reboot.
It can take a long time to download a whole program from the Internet.

3. Tools

file — a flat metal tool with a rough surface, used to make edges smooth; axe — a tool with a heavy blade for chopping wood; screwdriver — a tool for turning screws.

corkscrew — a tool with a spiral point for pulling corks out of bottles; needle — a thin pointed tool for sewing (used with thread); saw — a tool with a long toothed blade for cutting wood.

hammer — a tool for hitting nails; scissors — a two-bladed cutting tool; spanner — a metal tool for turning nuts; spade — a tool for digging.

Related words: spare parts — extra parts kept for repairs; tools — equipment in general; blunt — not sharp (a blunt knife needs to be sharpened); manufacturer — the company that makes something; a machine that runs on batteries gets its power from batteries; set an alarm clock — to make it ring at a chosen time.

You can make the edges smooth with a file.
This nut is impossible to undo. I need a larger spanner.
This knife is really blunt. I'll have to sharpen it.

4. Phrasal verbs

keep up with — to stay at the same speed or level as someone; run out — to be used up and finished (batteries, petrol); pick up — to receive a radio signal.

break down — (of a vehicle or machine) to stop working; put off — to discourage someone; hang up — to end a phone call.

look out — be careful!; do without — to manage when you do not have something; stand for — (of letters) to be a short form of; go off — (of food) to become bad; knock down — to demolish a building.

Common short phrases: turn off — to switch something off; plug in — to connect to the electricity supply; lock up — to lock a house or shop before leaving; consist of — to be made up of; be made of — to have something as its material.

My car isn't as fast as yours. I won't be able to keep up with you.
We'd better stop for some petrol. We've nearly run out.
The letters DVD stand for digital versatile disc.

⭐ Key points

  1. engine produces the power; a machine is equipment in general. electric = worked by electricity; electrical = to do with electricity.
  2. out of order = not working; out of work = unemployed. A power cut is a loss of electricity.
  3. invent = create something new; discover = find something already there.
  4. Computers: cursor, icon, highlight, reboot, scroll down, pull-down menu, search engine, download.
  5. Tools: file, axe, screwdriver, corkscrew, needle, saw, hammer, scissors, spanner, spade.
  6. Phrasal verbs: break down = stop working; run out = be finished; go off = (food) become bad; hang up = end a call; stand for = be a short form of.
