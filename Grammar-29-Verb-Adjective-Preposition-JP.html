1. 動詞の後の前置詞（Prepositions following verbs）

英語の動詞の多くは、決まった前置詞とセットで使われます。「動詞＋前置詞」の組み合わせごと覚えるのが一番の近道です：

about

agree about · argue about · boast about · dream about · know about · laugh about · read about · talk about
George and I argued about politics all night!
ジョージと私は一晩中政治について議論した！

at

guess at · laugh at · look at

for

apply for · arrange for · ask for · blame someone for · care for · forgive someone for · look for · pay for · search for · vote for · wait for
Over a hundred people applied for the job.
その仕事には100人以上が応募した。

in

believe in · confide in someone · involve someone in something · specialise in · succeed in · take part in
We succeeded in persuading Carol to lend us her boat.
私たちはキャロルを説得してボートを貸してもらうことに成功した。

of

accuse someone of · (dis)approve of · die of · dream of · remind someone of · rob someone of · smell of · taste of · warn someone of

on

blame something on someone · concentrate on · congratulate someone on · depend on · insist on · rely on
Joe congratulated Tony on passing the exam.
ジョーは試験に合格したトニーを祝福した。

to

add something to · admit to · apologise to someone for something · be accustomed to · be used to · belong to · confess to · explain something to someone · lend something to someone · listen to · object to · reply to · talk to someone about something
This car belongs to me.
この車は私のものだ。

with

agree with · argue with · begin with · charge someone with · deal with · discuss something with someone · provide someone with · share something with someone · trust someone with

2. 形容詞の後の前置詞（Prepositions following adjectives）

形容詞にも、決まった前置詞がセットで付きます：

about

annoyed about · anxious about · certain about · excited about · happy about · pleased about · right about · sorry about · upset about

at

angry at · annoyed at · bad at · good at · surprised at
Peter is good at drawing.
ピーターは絵を描くのが上手だ。

by

bored by · shocked by · surprised by

for

famous for · late for · ready for · sorry for
France is famous for its food.
フランスは食べ物で有名だ。

from

absent from · different from · safe from

in

interested in

of

afraid of · ashamed of · aware of · capable of · fond of · full of · jealous of · it is good of you (to do something)
Harry is afraid of snakes.
ハリーはヘビが怖い。

on

keen on

to

grateful to · kind to · married to

with

angry with · annoyed with · bored with · happy with · pleased with
⚠️ 同じ形容詞でも前置詞が変わると意味が変わることがあります（例：surprised at / surprised by、angry at / angry with、sorry about / sorry for）。Key points を確認しましょう。

⭐ Key points — 重要ポイント

  1. 前置詞は辞書で確認すること：新しい動詞や形容詞を学んだら、どの前置詞が続くかを辞書で確かめましょう。
  2. 前置詞が変われば意味も変わることがあります：同じ動詞・形容詞でも、前置詞によって意味が異なる場合があります。
  3. used to と be used to の違い：I used to go to the beach every weekend when I was a child. — 今はもうない過去の習慣で、ここの to は不定詞の一部（to go）です。一方 I am not used to getting up so early in the morning. の be used to は「〜に慣れている」という意味で、to は前置詞なので、後ろには動名詞（getting up）が来ます。
