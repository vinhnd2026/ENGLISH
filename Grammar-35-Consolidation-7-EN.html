🔁 Review — What this lesson covers

This final consolidation unit has no new grammar. It brings together four recent areas: inversion and question tags, linking words, pronouns and spelling.

a) Inversion and question tags

Not only / Only then / Seldom / Not once + auxiliary + subject + verb  ·  …, should one? / …, have they?

When a sentence begins with a negative or limiting phrase (Not only, Only then, Seldom, Not once…), the auxiliary verb moves before the subject, as in a question. A question tag must match the main clause in tense and pronoun: Nobody's got to stay late this evening, have they?

b) Linking words

First of all · After that · As a result · In conclusion · Personally

Linking words organise ideas by sequence and logic: opening (First of all), continuing (After that), result (As a result), concluding (In conclusion) and giving a personal view (Personally).

c) Pronouns

whoever / whatever = anyone / anything that…  ·  myself, yourself (reflexive)  ·  There is / are

Whoever, whatever, whenever, wherever mean "any person / thing / time / place that…". Reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself…) are used when the subject and object are the same person: Did you enjoy yourself? The structure There is/are introduces the existence of people and things.

d) Spelling

try → tried but trying  ·  prefer → preferred  ·  receive / relief (i before e, except after c)

Remember three frequent traps: -y changes to -i before -s/-ed but not before -ing; final consonants double when the stress is on the last syllable; and the ie/ei rule.

Not once did Jean offer her boss a word of apology.
(inversion after Not once)
Seldom have I had a more relaxing holiday.
One really shouldn't drink too much, should one?
(question tag)
Whoever had worn it before had certainly never washed it!
There were also what I later discovered to be pigs' ears scattered around the place.
First of all, I was given a greasy overall.
As a result, there was soon a huge pile of boxes waiting to be moved.
Did you enjoy yourself at the party?
(reflexive pronoun: yourself)

⭐ Key points

  1. Inversion after a negative or limiting phrase at the start of a sentence: Not only was my overall filthy…; Seldom have I had…
  2. Question tags match the main clause in tense and pronoun: …should one? …have they?
  3. Linking words by function: First of all · After that · As a result · In conclusion · Personally.
  4. Whoever / whatever mean “anyone / anything that…”; use a reflexive pronoun when subject and object are the same: enjoy yourself.
  5. Spelling: y → i before -s/-ed (tried) but not before -ing (trying); consonant doubling (preferred); ie/ei (receive, relief).
