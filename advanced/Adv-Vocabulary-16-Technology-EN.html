1. Naming devices — machines and technology words

contraption · gadget · appliance · equipment · machinery · component · manual · automation · overhaul · experiment

A contraption is a strange-looking device whose purpose is not obvious; a gadget is a small, clever, useful device. An appliance is a household electrical machine (a washing-machine, a fridge…).

Equipment and machinery are both uncountable: equipment is the set of devices needed for a purpose, while machinery means machines in general, especially large industrial ones.

A component is one part of a machine; a manual is the instruction book; automation is the use of machines instead of people; an overhaul is a complete check and repair; an experiment is a scientific test.

What a peculiar contraption! What on earth is that for?
(a strange device)
A washing-machine is probably the most useful household appliance.
(a household electrical machine)
My brother has a shop selling photographic equipment.
(equipment is uncountable)

2. Electricity words — wires, plugs and power

plug · socket · cable · lead · wire · fuse · mains · live · powered by · run by

A plug fits into a socket on the wall. A cable or lead connects a device to the power supply; a wire is the metal thread inside it.

Live means carrying electricity — dangerous. A fuse breaks the circuit when the current is too strong. Powered by / run by tells you what energy source a device uses.

Distinguish: a motor is electric, an engine burns fuel, and a machine is a device in general. The adjective is electrical (electrical engineer), the noun is electricity, and an electrician is the person who works with it.

Don't touch that wire! It's live.
(live = carrying electricity)
This small vacuum cleaner is run by batteries.
(run by = powered by)
The appliance is powered by a small electric motor.
(a motor, not an engine)

3. Tools and their parts

pliers · spanner · screwdriver · hammer · chisel · grinder · mixer · blender · blade · point · edge · bolt · nut · screw

Pliers grip and twist wires; a spanner turns nuts and bolts; a screwdriver turns screws.

In the kitchen: a grinder crushes things into powder (coffee beans), a mixer mixes ingredients, and a blender turns food into liquid.

On a knife: the blade is the flat cutting part, the point is the sharp tip, and the edge is the cutting side.

The electrician twisted the wires together using a pair of pliers.
(pliers for gripping and twisting)
I can't undo this nut. I need a larger spanner.
(a spanner turns nuts and bolts)

4. Word formation in technical writing

INSTALL → installation · MAINTAIN → maintenance · ADJUST → adjustable · ELECTRIC → electrical · APPLY → appliance · CYLINDER → cylindrical · TIGHT → tighten · SAFE → safety · PROTECT → protective · PROCEED → procedure

Technical manuals are full of derived words: nouns ending in -tion / -ance (installation, maintenance) and adjectives ending in -able / -ical / -ive (adjustable, cylindrical, protective).

Pairs to learn individually: tight → tighten (the verb), safe → safety, proceed → procedure, and apply → appliance — a device “applied” to a purpose.

Regular maintenance from a qualified engineer is advised.
(MAINTAIN → maintenance)
All engineers must observe all necessary safety precautions.
(SAFE → safety)

5. Problems and solutions — “It needs -ing” and fixed phrases

It needs oiling / recharging / sharpening / tightening / servicing / painting / adjusting / replacing / winding up / re-programming.

Need + -ing has a passive meaning: It needs oiling = it needs to be oiled. Each problem has its own solution: a squeaking door → oiling, a dead battery → recharging, a blunt pencil → sharpening, a loose screw → tightening.

Fixed phrases from the reading text: an aid to convenience, the very reason (= the exact reason), at someone's beck and call, on the strict understanding that…, to the point where…, and fend for yourself.

The battery is dead. It needs recharging.
(needs recharging = needs to be recharged)
Parents lose their freedom and the children lose the ability to fend for themselves.
(fend for themselves = look after themselves)

⭐ Key points

  1. Equipment and machinery are uncountable; an appliance is a household electrical machine, a gadget a small clever device, a contraption a strange-looking one.
  2. A plug fits a socket; live means carrying electricity; a fuse protects the circuit; devices are powered by / run by an energy source.
  3. A motor is electric, an engine burns fuel; electrical (adjective) ≠ electrician (the person) ≠ electricity (the noun).
  4. Tools: pliers grip and twist, a spanner turns nuts and bolts, a screwdriver turns screws; a knife has a blade, a point and an edge.
  5. Technical word formation: installation, maintenance, adjustable, electrical, appliance, cylindrical, tighten, safety, protective, procedure.
  6. Need + -ing is passive in meaning: It needs recharging = it needs to be recharged; each problem takes its own solution verb.
